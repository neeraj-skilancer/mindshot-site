Mindshot privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Mindshot ("the app") is a learning app published by Solset AI. This policy covers what the app and this website (mindshot.life) do with your information.

What the app collects

Nothing leaves your phone. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no servers.

The app stores these on your device only, so it can remember your progress:

  • The topics you picked
  • Your language, daily goal and reminder time
  • The ideas you have read and saved
  • Your recall answers and streak

This website

The app itself collects nothing. This website, mindshot.life, uses Google Analytics and PostHog to count visits and see which pages and links people use, for example how many visitors tap "Get it on Google Play". These tools are only switched on if you click Accept in the cookie banner. If you decline, nothing is tracked.

If you accept, these tools set cookies and record things like the pages you view, the links you click, your browser and device type, and your approximate location based on your IP address. We use this only to understand and improve the website. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

You can change your choice any time with Cookie settings. Google's use of this data is covered by Google's privacy policy, and PostHog's by PostHog's privacy policy.

Text-to-speech

When you tap Listen, your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine reads the card aloud. The app sends no audio or text to us.

Deleting your data

Go to Profile → Start over, or uninstall the app. Either one removes all data.

Children

Mindshot is for adults 18 and over.

Changes

If a future version adds accounts, sync, payments or analytics, we will update this policy before that version is released.
