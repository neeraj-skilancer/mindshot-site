Mindshot privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Mindshot ("the app") is a learning app published by Solset AI. This policy covers what the app and this website (mindshot.life) do with your information.

What the app collects

From version 1.2.0, the app creates a Mindshot account for you the first time you open it, so your progress can be backed up and so we can see how the app is used. You don't need to sign up: the account is anonymous unless you choose to sign in with Google.

We store this with your account:

  • A random account ID, your member number and whether you are a founding member
  • The topics and books you picked, your language, daily goal and reminder time
  • The ideas you have read and saved, your recall answers and your streak
  • Daily activity: how many cards you read, recalled, saved and shared each day
  • App events, such as opening the app, finishing setup, saving or sharing a card
  • Your phone type (Android or web) and app version
  • If you sign in with Google: your email address

We use this to back up and restore your progress, to show founding-member status, and to understand which topics and features help people learn so we can improve the app. We do not sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising. The app has no ads.

The data is stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud) and is encrypted in transit. Only the Mindshot team can see it. Google's handling is covered by Firebase's privacy information.

This website

This website, mindshot.life, uses Google Analytics and PostHog to count visits and see which pages and links people use, for example how many visitors tap "Get it on Google Play". These tools are only switched on if you click Accept in the cookie banner. If you decline, nothing is tracked.

If you accept, these tools set cookies and record things like the pages you view, the links you click, your browser and device type, and your approximate location based on your IP address. We use this only to understand and improve the website. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

You can change your choice any time with Cookie settings. Google's use of this data is covered by Google's privacy policy, and PostHog's by PostHog's privacy policy.

Text-to-speech

When you tap Listen, your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine reads the card aloud. The app sends no audio or text to us.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Profile → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account and everything stored with it, on our servers and on your phone. You can also request deletion on the web. Profile → Start over only clears your progress on this phone and keeps your account.

We keep your data until you delete your account. Accounts that have not been used for 24 months may be deleted.

Children

Mindshot is for adults 18 and over.

Changes

If a future version adds payments or ads, we will update this policy before that version is released.
