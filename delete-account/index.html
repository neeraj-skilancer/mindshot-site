Delete your Mindshot account

Mindshot by Solset AI. You can delete your account and all the data stored with it at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Mindshot and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete my account, then Delete.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email skilancersolar@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Mindshot account". Include the Google email you signed in with, or your member number (shown in Profile). We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account ID, member number and founding-member status
  • Your topics, books, language, goal and reminder settings
  • Your read and saved ideas, recall answers, streak and daily activity
  • All app events linked to your account
  • Your Google email, if you signed in with Google

Nothing is kept, except totals that can't identify you (for example, how many people signed up on a given day). Uninstalling the app does not delete your account; use one of the options above.
