Questions

Is Mindshot free?

Yes. Mindshot is free to download and use on Android.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app, pick your topics and start swiping. Your progress is saved on your phone.

How long does a session take?

You set a daily goal of 5, 10 or 15 minutes. The feed ends when you reach it, with one thing to try that day.

Which languages can I use?

English, Hinglish and Hindi today, with more on the way. You can switch language on any card.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. Mindshot is on Android first.

What data do you collect?

The app saves your progress to an anonymous Mindshot account, so it can be backed up and restored, and counts how the app is used. You can sign in with Google to keep your progress on a new phone, and delete your account any time from Profile. There are no ads, and we never sell your data. This website uses cookies to count visits, but only if you accept them. Read the privacy policy.
