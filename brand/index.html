Mindshot brand kit

Use these files and rules for anything with the Mindshot name on it: posts, Reels, ads, store graphics and the app. The mark is a stack of idea cards with a spark of insight on top. Tagline: One idea. Every swipe. Support line: 5 minutes a day.

Voice

A sharp, friendly senior colleague: short, specific, a little playful, never preachy. Lead with the useful bit and use examples that make sense anywhere. Mindshot is language-based, not country-based: each language (English, Hinglish, Hindi and more later) gets copy written natively for its readers, not translated word for word.

Use: idea, swipe, 5 minutes, sharper, try today, remember. Avoid: hustle, guru, hack your brain, life-changing, revolution, guaranteed.
